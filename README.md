 How I Think

I believe talent is everywhere; opportunity is not.

Growing up in an environment where electricity, reliable internet, mentorship, and technology resources were limited shaped the way I think about products.

I don't want to build technology simply because it can be built.

I want to understand:

Who has the problem?
Why does it matter?
What are they doing today?
What would actually make their lives better?

Then I build.
